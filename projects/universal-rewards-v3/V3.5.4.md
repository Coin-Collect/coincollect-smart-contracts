## Defensive review

| Surface | Verified behavior and evidence | Residual check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Treasury and solvency | The ledger is the only vault allocation and accounting caller. Withdrawals use the available ledger balance. Exact transfer checks and reserve accounting isolate reward inventory and obligations per pool and token. `treasury.test.cjs`, `funding.test.cjs` and the pinned Treasury fork exercise deposits, withdrawals, spends, returns and permission boundaries. | Validate selected token proxy governance and behavior. Unsolicited transfers have no accounting credit. Confirm recovery and operational balance monitoring with reviewed addresses. |
| Claims, deadlines and recovery | Current ownership is checked before payouts. Settling stops at pool end. The exclusive cutoff is `end + 30 days`, including paying exits. Funding recovery and reward recovery are separate and return to the immutable Treasury or its attached vault. Existing settlement tests cover expiry boundaries, ownership transfers, rounding and multi-pool isolation. | Externally review rounding and accounting invariants under longer fuzz runs and selected ERC-20 failure behaviors. |
| ERC-721 and managed units | Bounded ERC-721 capability and ownership reads. Managed ERC-1155 requires explicit approval, pinned compatible collection runtime and its bound synchronous hook. Managed positions checkpoint and apportion rights on quantity changes. Tests cover transfers, partial exits, repeat settlement, adapter boundaries and scale. | Certify actual collection implementations and proxy upgrade controls. A failing synchronous hook can revert the NFT transfer. Exercise worst-case real collection hook gas before approval. |
| Pricing and ordering | Router hash, factory/pair identity, minimum reserves, maximum input, observed price bounds, deadline and epoch accounting protect each settlement. Both direct and two-hop paths are tested on real pinned Polygon bytecode in chain 31337. Repeated successful replenishment for the same lane and epoch is rejected. | A market with thin reserves can still be unsuitable. Calibrate limits, review MEV exposure and monitor actual TWAP freshness on the final deployment. |
| Privileges and automation | Unified admin controls the vault, pool creation and route governance. Permissionless automation uses a separate POL-only executor. No executor role can withdraw Treasury funds. Production execution requires explicit environment authorization. | Confirm hardware or multisig policy, executor balance caps, alerting, rotation, failure drills and incident paging. Reverted transactions or competing workers can still cost gas. |
| Gas and batching | Protocol caps and paging bound work. Scale tests cover 32, 128, 512 and 1,024 positions and inventory above 1,024. The frozen release supports per-position claim and exit. The experimental local batch selectors are absent from the release ABI. | Measure deployed collection hooks with actual transaction types and validate that large transfer batches respect collection-specific gas budgets. |
| Remote NFT rights | Base and Arbitrum verifier artifacts are isolated. Polygon deployment sequence omits remote journal and verifier configuration. | Independent review of signer quorum, liveness and finality is needed before any remote participation. |

The RPC reported chain 137 and block hash `0xc7ae491e11ac6ca85c3d783cedc78229688549259e7d315bd4d0cbe1346ed8fe`. The snapshot records router, factory, pair, asset and discovered implementation runtime hashes, visible roles, pair reserves and cumulative counters. The primary markets inspected were CoinCollect V2 and QuickSwap V2 on Polygon. The latter was used for real routing in the pinned fork.

USDC exposes EIP-1967 implementation/admin and roles for the owner, minter administration, pausing and blacklisting. USDT, USDC.e and SHIB expose a proxy implementation and proxy owner by call. Establish exact implementation/proxy structure and authority against verified source before mainnet approval. Asset hashes and balances cannot independently prove future token behavior.

### Gas economics

The isolated fork measured 319,774 gas to join, 501,551 to Harvest, 384,548 for a WPOL purchase, 623,377 for COLLECT and 625,904 for SHIB, plus 689,044 across eight observations. At a live gas-price reading of 278.752156983 gwei and an illustrative 1 WPOL quote of 0.075760 USDT, join plus Harvest was about 0.229 POL / 0.017344 USDT. A three-token purchase and observation window modeled about 0.648 POL / 0.049055 USDT. Thirteen modeled windows are about 8.418 POL / 0.637715 USDT, around 6.38%, 0.64% and 0.064% of 10, 100 and 1,000 USDT, respectively. This aggregate gas model assumes successful windows. Actual workers can defer on per-purchase bounds. Modeling windows at gas prices observed later than the snapshot is an estimate.
